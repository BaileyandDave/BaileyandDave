Welcome to the world of Bailey & Dave!

📚 About the Books

The Bailey & Dave adventures are interactive picture books designed to encourage children to join in, explore and use their imagination.

  • 👀 Searching for clues
  • 🔢 Counting challenges
  • 🐾 Joining in with the story
  • 💭 Exploring new adventures

🎮 About the Activities

The website includes free activities inspired by Bailey and Dave's adventures.

  • 🐾 Paw Print Mazes
  • 🔎 Finding games
  • 🎒 Adventure challenges
  • ✏️ Printable activities

More activities will appear as Bailey and Dave discover new places.

🔒 Safe & Family Friendly

The Bailey & Dave website is designed with children and families in mind.

  • No personal information is needed
  • No chat features
  • No accounts required
  • No advertising aimed at children

🐶 Meet Bailey & Dave

Bailey
A lovable miniature dachshund who can be nervous when he discovers something unfamiliar.

Dave
A brave and cheeky pug who is always ready for an adventure.

📖 Learning Through Adventure

The books and activities can encourage:

  • Early reading skills
  • Observation
  • Counting practice
  • Imagination
  • Confidence
