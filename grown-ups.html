🏠 Home

Welcome to Bailey & Dave

Bailey & Dave is an interactive children's picture book series about two best friends — Bailey the silly sausage and Dave the cheeky pug.

The stories invite children to become part of each adventure by helping the dogs, spotting things in the illustrations, counting, searching and, of course, giving the occasional lucky ear tickle.

📖 Why Interactive?

Bailey & Dave stories are designed to make reading something children participate in rather than simply watch.

Readers might be asked to find something, count objects, solve a little problem or help Bailey realise that his latest "scary alien" might not be quite as scary as he thinks.

🐶 Meet the Dogs

Bailey is a miniature dachshund with a very big imagination. He's our lovable silly sausage.

Dave is his cheeky pug best friend — curious, playful and usually a little braver when something unexpected appears.

🎨 Activities & Downloads

The Bailey & Dave website is growing alongside the books.

Free colouring pages, puzzles, certificates and other printable activities will gradually be added so children can continue their adventures after the story has finished.

Printing and downloading should be done with the help of a grown-up.

🔐 A Child-Friendly Website

The Bailey & Dave website is being designed so children can explore the world of the books without needing their own account.

Activities are intended to be enjoyed with appropriate grown-up involvement, particularly where downloading or printing is required.

✍️ About the Author

Bailey & Dave is written by children's author Jack Smith.

The series began with a simple idea: create funny, warm adventures where the child reading the book becomes part of the story.

Each new adventure introduces somewhere new to explore, something new to discover and another memory for Bailey and Dave to bring home.

🖼️ The Memory Wall

Every adventure leaves a memory behind.

Bailey and Dave's Memory Wall remembers the friends, places and adventures they have shared with their readers.

As the series grows, the Memory Wall will grow too.
