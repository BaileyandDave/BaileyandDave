Say Hello!

Meet Bailey and Dave — two best friends who somehow manage to find an adventure wherever they go.

Along the way they meet lots of new friends... although Bailey isn't always quite so sure about them at first!

🐕

Bailey

The Silly Sausage

Bailey is a miniature dachshund with a big imagination.

Strange noises, mysterious creatures and unexpected visitors can sometimes mean only one thing...

"It's a scary alien!"

Luckily, Bailey has Dave — and you — to help discover what's really going on.

🐶

Dave

The Cheeky Pug

Dave is Bailey's best friend and usually the braver of the two.

He's curious, playful and always ready to investigate whatever strange thing Bailey has decided must be an alien.

Of course, Dave has his own priorities too...

Especially when somebody mentions the T-word!
