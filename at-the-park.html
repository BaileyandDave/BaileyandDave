Bailey and Dave at the Park

A visit to the park becomes another adventure for one silly sausage and one cheeky pug.

Children explore the outdoors with Bailey and Dave as the two lovable dogs encounter new friends, solve little challenges and discover that things are not always as frightening as Bailey first imagines.

More than just listening

The reader becomes part of the story through gentle prompts to look, count, search and help the characters along the way.
