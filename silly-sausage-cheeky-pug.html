The first Bailey & Dave adventure

Bailey and Dave: Silly Sausage and Cheeky Pug

Meet Bailey, a timid miniature dachshund, and Dave, his brave and cheerful pug friend.

This interactive children's picture book invites young readers to join the two dogs rather than simply listen. Children help them explore, look for clues and become part of the adventure.

Join in with the story

Bailey expects unfamiliar things to be scary. Dave is ready to investigate. With a little help from the reader, the best friends discover that a frightening surprise may simply be a new friend waiting to be met.
