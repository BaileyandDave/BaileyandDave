Meet Bailey & Dave

Best friends with very different ideas about what counts as scary.

Bailey — The Silly Sausage

Timid, lovable and not very good at hiding

Bailey is a miniature dachshund who hears an unfamiliar animal noise and immediately expects the worst. Luckily, his scary aliens usually turn into new friends.

“It’s a scary alien!”
Dave — The Cheeky Pug

Brave, calm and always ready to investigate

Dave is Bailey’s best friend. He encourages Bailey, helps solve each adventure and never makes fun of him for being nervous.

Adventure first. T-R-E-A-T-S afterwards.
